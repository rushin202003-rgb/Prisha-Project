# PRD-007 — Functional Feature Requirements (Part 3)

## F-007 Revision Planner
Uses exam dates, syllabus, difficulty, confidence, available study time, school schedule, and revision buffer.

The current requirement is to reserve the final 12 days before exams for revision.

## F-008 Exam Countdown
Shows upcoming exams, countdowns, and positive readiness indicators.

## F-009 Hobby Hub
Interests:
- Drawing
- Dancing
- Music
- Storybooks
- Crafts
- Badminton
- Puzzles
- Travel planning
- Games
- Knitting/crochet

Hobbies are scheduled rather than treated as disposable extras.

## F-010 Journal & Mood Diary
Mood, journal entries, gratitude, achievements, and reflection. Journal content is private by default.
