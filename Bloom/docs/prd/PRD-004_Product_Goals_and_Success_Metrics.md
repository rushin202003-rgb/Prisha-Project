# PRD-004 — Product Goals & Success Metrics

## North Star
Meaningful Daily Learning Sessions Completed.

## Goals
1. Reduce study stress.
2. Build consistent study habits.
3. Improve academic organisation.
4. Protect balance.
5. Increase student confidence.

## Measurement principle
Do not optimise for screen time or notification volume. Measure learning, balance, wellbeing, confidence, and healthy routines.
