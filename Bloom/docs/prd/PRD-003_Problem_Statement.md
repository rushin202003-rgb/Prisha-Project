# PRD-003 — Problem Statement & Opportunity Analysis

## Primary problem
Students often know what they need to study but struggle with when, how, and how much to study while still making time for hobbies, play, rest, and family.

## Pain points
- Time management
- Exam preparation
- Homework and notes
- Worksheets
- Emotional pressure
- Different learning styles

## Opportunity
Combine planning, learning, wellbeing, and personal growth into one student-first platform.
