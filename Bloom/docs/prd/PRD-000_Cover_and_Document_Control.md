# PRD-000 — Cover & Document Control

**Project:** Project Bloom  
**Version:** 1.0.0 Draft  
**Status:** In Progress  

## Purpose
Master product specification for Project Bloom.

## Vision
Help students learn with curiosity, organise with confidence, grow through healthy habits, and enjoy childhood.

## Target
Students in Grades 5–8, initially ICSE, with an iPad-first experience.

## Product principles
1. Childhood should not be sacrificed for productivity.
2. AI should encourage rather than judge.
3. Learning should be personalised.
4. Healthy routines matter more than perfect schedules.
5. Progress should be celebrated.
