# PRD-007 — Functional Feature Requirements (Part 1)

## F-001 Home Dashboard
Central student screen containing greeting, date, mood, today's schedule, homework, notes, worksheets, hobby spotlight, exams, progress, achievements, quick actions, AI recommendation, and tomorrow preview.

## F-002 Smart Scheduler
Inputs:
- School schedule
- Homework
- Notes
- Worksheets
- Exams
- Revision goals
- Hobbies
- Play time
- Mood/energy
- Parent preferences

Generate three options:
- Balanced
- Exam Focus
- Relaxed

Protect sleep, meals, play, hobbies, and family time. Explain changes and do not silently replace plans.
