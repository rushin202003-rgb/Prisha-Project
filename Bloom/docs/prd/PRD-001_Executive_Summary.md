# PRD-001 — Executive Summary

Project Bloom is an AI-powered Student Life Companion designed to help students manage academic responsibilities while preserving hobbies, play, creativity, family time, and rest.

## Core capabilities
- Intelligent study scheduling
- Adaptive replanning
- AI tutoring
- Homework tracking
- Notes and worksheet tracking
- Exam and revision planning
- Hobby management
- Journal and mood diary
- Rewards and achievements
- Parent progress view

## Version 1
Focus on the core student experience and parent progress view. Teacher and school administration capabilities are future scope.
