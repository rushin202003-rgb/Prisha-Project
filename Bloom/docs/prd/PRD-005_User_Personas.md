# PRD-005 — User Personas

## Primary student
Representative profile:
- Class 6
- ICSE
- iPad-first
- Curious, creative, energetic
- Enjoys drawing, dancing, music, storybooks, crafts, badminton, puzzles, travel planning, games, and knitting/crochet

## Parent/guardian
Needs meaningful progress visibility, homework/revision status, healthy routines, less repetitive reminding, and respect for student independence.

## Future teacher
Future features may include homework sharing, worksheets, revision recommendations, and classroom coordination.

## AI companion
Friendly, patient, supportive, age-appropriate, and original rather than imitating a real person.
