# PRD-002 — Product Vision

## Vision
Project Bloom will become a trusted AI-powered Student Life Companion that helps students learn with curiosity, organise with confidence, grow through healthy habits, and enjoy every stage of childhood.

## Values
- Joy before pressure
- Balance over burnout
- Encourage, never shame
- Personalisation
- Explain AI decisions
- Student independence

## Long-term direction
Grow from a study planner into a broader life dashboard covering learning, hobbies, creativity, reading, travel planning, goals, achievements, journaling, and wellbeing.
